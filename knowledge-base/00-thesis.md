# 00 · Thesis — Aultrum is built on Persona Intelligence

> **One sentence:**
> Aultrum is a Persona Intelligence company. The judgement it computerises is: **is quality care being delivered, and is it consistent?**

---

## The judgement

Across care there is one question every decision, every process, and every system ultimately answers:

> **Is quality care being delivered to this person — and is it being delivered consistently?**

- **Quality** is measured through the six Aultrum Layers (Competence, Compliance, Shift Readiness, Rapport, Satisfaction, Purpose).
- **Consistency** is the temporal dimension — quality held over time. In industry terms this consistency is called **retention**: of carers, of clients, of relationships, of standards.

Quality without consistency is a moment. Consistency without quality is a routine. Aultrum computerises the judgement that holds both together.

---

## What Persona Intelligence is

Persona Intelligence is the learning layer of the system. It is built on:

1. **Research theory** — every Layer is grounded in its own legal and psychological frameworks.
2. **Mathematical models** — frameworks are translated into graded qualifiers using formal mathematical models.
3. **Observational learning** — live package data refines the theory over time, correcting and perfecting the research base from what is actually observed.

Persona Intelligence is not a profile. It is a **continuous, evolving judgement** of a person or package, sharpened every time care happens.

---

## How the Layers produce the judgement

| Lens | Observes | Feeds the judgement |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| Competence | Is the skill current and verified? | Quality |
| Compliance | Are legal obligations met? | Quality |
| Shift Readiness | Is the carer fit to deliver today? | Quality |
| Rapport | Is the relationship healthy? | Quality |
| Satisfaction | Does the receiver feel cared for? | Quality |
| Purpose | Is the work meaningful to the carer? | Consistency |

The judgement unifies them: quality across all six lenses, held consistent over time.

## Why this is the moat

- A competitor can copy a rewards system, a scheduler, or a voice agent.
- They cannot copy six framework-grounded lenses, each with legal + psychological bases, each graded by math, each refined by observation over time.
- The compounding asset is the **accumulated, structure-weighted evidence of quality delivered consistently** — a dataset that only exists because judgement was computerised.
