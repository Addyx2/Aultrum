Capabilities

Built for compliance. Designed for care.

Auto-Generated Care Plans

AI produces fully compliant care plans from resident profiles, assessments, and daily observations. Structured to CQC standards from the first draft.

Compliance Auditing

Continuous, real-time checks against regulatory frameworks. Catches gaps before they become inspection findings. Flags missing signatures, overdue reviews, and incomplete records.

Version Control

Every change tracked with user, timestamp, and diff. Full audit trail from first draft to final sign-off. Roll back to any previous version instantly.

Inspection Reports

Generate CQC-ready inspection reports in minutes. Collates evidence, populates frameworks, and produces submission-ready documents with supporting data.

Voice-to-Document

Dictate observations, handover notes, or incident reports directly. Transcription converts speech to structured, formatted documents in real time.

System Integration

Connects to existing care management platforms, EPRs, and scheduling systems via API. Bi-directional sync keeps records consistent across your stack.
