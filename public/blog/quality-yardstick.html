The Quality Yardstick

Why is quality care being delivered — and is it consistent? It is the right question to automate.

Aultrum is built on the premise that the hardest and most important question in social care is: is quality care being delivered, and is it consistent?

Most systems record activity. The Quality Yardstick measures judgement.

Persona Intelligence learns from expert decisions to codify that standard and apply it continuously.
