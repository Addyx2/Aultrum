Data processing agreement

How Aultrum Technologies Ltd (the “processor”) handles personal data for the organisations that use its products (each a “controller”), as UK GDPR Article 28 requires. It forms part of our terms of service.

1. What we process

  • Subject matter and purpose: providing Elements, Pleasant and Aultrum ID to the controller.
  • Duration: while the controller uses our products, and up to 30 days after, to return or delete the data.
  • People: the controller’s staff and carers, its clients and their contacts, and people who call, text or chat with it through Gateway.
  • Data: names, contact details, work records (shifts, timesheets, training and compliance records, pay), Peak points and rewards, and conversation transcripts. Compliance records may include health information or criminal record check results where the controller enters them.

2. Our obligations

  • Process the data only on the controller’s documented instructions, which are these terms and the controller’s use of the product, unless the law requires otherwise; we’ll say so if we think an instruction breaks the law.
  • Make sure everyone who can access the data is bound to keep it confidential.
  • Keep it secure with appropriate technical and organisational measures (section 3).
  • Use sub-processors only as set out in section 4.
  • Help the controller answer people who use their data protection rights, and with security, breach notifications, impact assessments and consultations with the ICO, taking into account what we can reasonably do.
  • At the end, delete or return the data as the controller chooses, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Give the controller the information needed to show we meet these obligations, and allow reasonable audits on 30 days’ notice, at most once a year unless there has been a breach.

3. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit; passwords hashed; secrets kept out of the code.
  • Access limited by role inside each organisation, and to the staff at Aultrum who need it to run the service.
  • One-time, expiring links for sign-in emails and invites; signed, expiring links for embedded screens; signed webhooks.
  • Rate limits on sign-in and public endpoints; account lock-out after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • An audit log of important actions in each organisation.

4. Sub-processors

The controller authorises us to use these sub-processors. We will give at least 30 days’ notice by email before adding or replacing one, so the controller can object; if we can’t resolve an objection, the controller may end the agreement. Each sub-processor is bound by data protection terms at least as protective as these.

5. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or another safeguard UK law allows.

6. Personal data breaches

We will tell the controller without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting their data, with what we know, what we are doing about it, and what they may need to do. We will keep them updated.

7. Contact

For anything about this agreement, email Wisdom@aultrum.co.uk.
