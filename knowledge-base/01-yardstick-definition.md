# 01 · The Yardstick — measuring unit of quality care

> **Working definition (refine over time):**
> A **yardstick for quality care** is a measurable observable — tied to a legal or psychological basis, graded by a mathematical model, and validated over time by persona intelligence — that tells us whether quality care is being delivered consistently.

---

## Anatomy of a yardstick

Every yardstick has four parts. If one part is missing, it is not yet a yardstick — it is an intention.

```
 1. OBSERVABLE
    What is seen or recorded in the ordinary course of care.
    (e.g. a shift clocked in, a training record, a praise note, a satisfaction reply)

 2. BASIS
    Why this observable earns the weight it is given:
    - legal framework  → the obligation it serves (statute, regulation, CQC duty)
    - psychological framework → the human effect it captures (fatigue, trust, motivation)

 3. MATHEMATICAL MODEL
    How the observable is graded into a value on the qualifier.
    (e.g. continuity index, decay curve, fatigue ratio, trend delta)

 4. VALIDATION
    How persona intelligence corrects the model from observed reality over time.
    (e.g. the model predicted risk; the data confirmed or refuted — the model adjusts)
```

---

## The formal definition, expanded

| Component | Question it answers | Example |
|-----------|--------------------|---------|
| **Observable** | What do we measure? | Clock-in time vs. rota start time |
| **Legal basis** | What are we obligated to check? | Right to safely staffed, consistent care |
| **Psychological basis** | What human process is at work? | Fatigue accumulation across consecutive shifts |
| **Mathematical model** | How is it graded? | Readiness score = f(shifts_in_window, rest_between_shifts, prior incidents) |
| **Persona validation** | How does reality correct theory? | Fatigue flagged twice, no incident → model recalibrates threshold |

---

## Levels of yardsticks

Not all yardsticks weigh equally. Three levels:

| Level | Name | Example | Use |
|-------|------|---------|-----|
| L1 | **Signal** | A single observation (clock-in, training record, praise note) | Raw input; never judged alone |
| L2 | **Measure** | A graded value from one mathematical model (readiness score, continuity index) | Qualifier input |
| L3 | **Judgement** | Cross-lens synthesis on a package (package health) | The delivered insight |

A qualifier **grades** L1 signals into L2 measures. Persona Intelligence **weights** L2 measures into L3 judgements.

---

## Why the yardstick is the core unit

- It is what makes quality **computerisable** — a testable, falsifiable statement about care.
- It is what makes quality **consistent** — the same observable, graded by the same model, every time.
- It is what makes quality **improving** — persona intelligence tightens each yardstick from observation.
- It is what makes quality **defensible** — every yardstick points back to a legal or psychological basis.

---

## The single judgement, restated with yardsticks

> A package delivers quality care consistently when **enough yardsticks, across enough lenses, hold at or above grade over time** — and persona intelligence confirms the grading matches observed reality.
