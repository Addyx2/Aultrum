One closed loop

From a shift request to a paid invoice, without re-keying anything.

Most agencies stitch together a rota tool, a timesheet app, payroll software and a spreadsheet of invoices. Pleasant is one record from start to finish, so your margin is visible the moment a carer clocks in.

  1. 01

    Request

    A care home or provider asks for cover, by phone or through their Pleasant Link.

  2. 02

    Match

    Pleasant suggests carers who are free, cleared to work and right for the role.

  3. 03

    Clock in

    Carers clock in and out on their phone against the real shift.

  4. 04

    Sign off

    The client's named signatory approves the hours. No sign-off, no invoice.

  5. 05

    Pay

    PAYE, NI and pension calculated from approved hours, with RTI and BACS files ready.

  6. 06

    Invoice

    The client invoice comes from the same hours, with your margin on every shift.

Oversight

Know what needs you before the phone rings.

Oversight opens on the problems, most urgent first, each with the button that fixes it.

  • Shifts starting in the next 48 hours with no carer, and unattended shifts
  • Carers who aren't cleared to work: missing or expired DBS or right to work
  • Clients missing a rate card or a timesheet signatory
  • Timesheets to approve, and overdue invoices for admins
  • Cover for the next 7 days, compared with the last 7

Clientpointe

Clients are organisations, not names on a list.

Every care home and provider has its units or branches, its contacts and its own rate card, so every shift is priced and signed off correctly.

  • Rate cards by role: day, night, weekend, bank holiday and a flat sleep-in charge
  • Rates for the whole client or for one unit
  • Named signatories approve timesheets through a 24-hour Pleasant Link
  • Framework or PO references and payment terms on every invoice

Payroll and billing

Pay carers and bill clients from the same approved hours.

Payroll and invoices come from one set of approved, client-signed timesheets, so they always agree.

  • UK PAYE, National Insurance and pension for every carer, with payslips
  • FPS-ready RTI file for HMRC, plus BACS STD18 and bank payment files
  • Client invoices with sleep-ins shown as flat charges, not hours
  • Xero sales-invoice export for your accounts

Persona Assist

Assist does the work. You approve it.

Ask in plain English: fill tomorrow's open shifts, chase unsigned timesheets, set up a new care home. Assist finds the answer in your live data and prepares the change. Nothing happens until someone approves it.

  • Every change waits on an Approve or Decline card, and is written to the audit log
  • Works within each person's role. Admins choose which areas Assist can read or change
  • Scheduled automations such as a morning briefing or a compliance watch, which can only read
  • Saved, searchable chat history, private to each user

Persona

Built on the first layers of quality care.

Persona maps every carer's abilities to every client's needs. Pleasant starts where care must start: is this carer capable, lawful and fit for this shift?

Layer I

Competence

Skills matched to this client's needs, not just credentials held. Anchored in the Care Certificate, the RQF, the Core Skills Training Framework and NICE guidance.

Layer II

Compliance

A gate, not a trade-off. DBS, right to work and training are checked on every carer, and gaps surface before anyone is booked.

Layer III

Shift Readiness

Protecting the carer's capacity to care is protecting the client. Pleasant already refuses overlapping bookings; fatigue-aware readiness is next.
