# 07 · Purpose — lens on the consistency of care

> **Lens:** Does this work give the carers in the package a sense of meaning, growth, and belonging — the conditions under which carers stay?

---

## Definition

Purpose observes the carer-side of the relationship. It is the inverse lens of Satisfaction: where Satisfaction asks how the receiver experiences care, Purpose asks whether the carers' work is meaningful to them — because carers who experience purpose deliver consistent care, and consistency is retention.

## Psychological framework(s)

> *[Placeholder — insert motivation/self-determination theory references.]*

Sketch of the basis:

- Recognized work builds meaning; unseen work decays it — recognition precedes retention.
- Identity: people stay where the work is part of who they are.
- Burnout as purpose erosion: sustained overload converts meaning into obligation.

## Mathematical grading model

> Working model — refine with the research base.

```
Purpose qualifier = f(tenure, growth, recognition, relationship) − burnout_adjust

  tenure      = persistence in the package over time
  growth      = participation in development activity
  recognition = recognition received and surfaced (valued, visible)
  relationship = positive peer/mentor/coordinator signals
  burnout_adjust = fatigue/overload deduction (from Shift Readiness co-signals)
```

Purpose is the **consistency predictor** — the lens most directly tied to whether carers remain and therefore whether the package stays whole.

---

## Persona Intelligence inputs

- Tenure, attendance, training and development events.
- Recognition receipt vs. recognition value (engagement, not just counts).
- Burnout/readiness co-signals.
- The engine learns: *which purpose signals, at which intensity, actually predict a carer staying* — the retention model sharpens per persona.
