04 // PERSONNEL ● HUMAN_IN_THE_LOOP

The human in the loop.

Personnel is how Gateway works with your people — alerting, informing, and stepping in whenever a call needs a human. It pairs with Abilities: when a booking, payment, or complex case needs a person, Gateway hands it to Personnel.

notifications_active

Alert

Urgent or sensitive calls trigger an instant alert to the right person. Falls, safeguarding, escalation — never missed.

info

Inform

Keep the right people in the loop — carers, managers, admissions — with the summary they need, when they need it.

support_agent

Take over

Live handover to a human any time. Gateway falls back so the caller always reaches a person when it matters.
