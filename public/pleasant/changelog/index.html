Changelog

What's new in Pleasant.

New features, improvements and fixes, as they ship.

24 October 2026

Persona, Assist and an action-driven Oversight

pleasant.aultrum.co.uk

  • New

    Needs attention

    Oversight now opens on every blocker, most urgent first: uncovered shifts, carers who aren't cleared to work, clients missing a rate card or signatory, overdue invoices and timesheets to approve. Each has the button that fixes it, and client-signed timesheets can be approved right there.

  • New

    Persona and Assist

    Persona is Pleasant's AI suite. Assist answers questions from live data and prepares changes for you to approve. It has a full chat page with saved history, scheduled read-only automations such as a morning briefing, and an admin page to choose what Assist can read or change.

  • New

    Clients as organisations

    Clients are now care homes and providers, with units or branches, contacts, named timesheet signatories and per-role rate cards for day, night, weekend, bank holiday and sleep-ins.

  • New

    Roles, team and audit log

    Admin and Manager roles, team invites, an audit log with CSV export, sign-in lockout, revocable sessions and a ⌘K command palette.

  • Improved

    KPIs with context

    Shifts today vs last week, cover for the next 7 days, the oldest timesheet waiting, and carers who joined this week.

  • Improved

    Sidebar and account menu

    Collapse the sidebar to icons, show or hide each section, and find Settings, Import and Add-ons under your account name.

  • Improved

    Sleep-ins and rota pricing

    Clients can be charged a separate flat sleep-in rate, and recurring rota shifts are priced from the client's rate card for each date.

  • Fixed

    Xero export

    Invoices now export to Xero as sales invoices with the correct amounts, UK tax rates and dates, instead of bills.

  • Fixed

    Sleep-in invoice lines

    Sleep-ins show as a flat charge on invoices and lender packs, not as hours at an hourly rate.

8 October 2026

Overnight shifts and safer imports

  • Fixed

    Overnight shifts

    Shifts that run past midnight are handled correctly.

  • Fixed

    Imports

    CSV imports run as a single transaction, so a bad row no longer leaves a half-finished import.

3 October 2026

Dispatch, rota, compliance and payroll files

  • New

    Dispatch and recurring rota

    Offer open shifts to carers, handle shift requests, and generate weeks from recurring rota templates.

  • New

    Compliance

    DBS, right to work and training expiry tracked for every carer.

  • New

    Payroll and invoicing files

    BACS payment files, Xero export, CSV import and agency settings.
