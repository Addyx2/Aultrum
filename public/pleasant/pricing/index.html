QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

ASK ABOUT PRICINGarrow_forward
Who counts as an active carer?add

Only carers who worked a shift in the month. Carers on your books who didn't work aren't charged.

Does Pleasant file RTI with HMRC?add

Pleasant calculates PAYE, NI and pension to current HMRC thresholds and produces an FPS-ready RTI file for you or your accountant to submit. Direct submission is being piloted with early agencies, and final liability remains with the agency that files.

Is Persona Assist included?add

Yes. Assist is part of Pleasant. It works within each person's role, every change it prepares waits for approval, and admins choose which areas it can read or change.

Can I bring my existing data?add

Yes. Workers and clients can be imported from CSV files.
