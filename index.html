Founding Team

Our founding team combines an on-field care experience of 25 years. Our boots-on-the-ground approach singles us out as practical industry stakeholders, and this core understanding gives us the advantage to address the pain points hindering the delivery of proactive care services.

Belief System

Care providers operate under immense pressure, managing complex operational logistics while trying to deliver life-changing services. We believe that technology should reduce this administrative burden, not add to it.

By bringing proactive AI into the care ecosystem, we aim to give your team the tools they need to focus on what truly matters: delivering exceptional care.

What we want to achieve

Our goal is simple.

We want to help care providers make better workforce decisions, reduce unnecessary paperwork, support their teams, and improve the quality of care people receive.

We start by understanding how your organization works and the challenges your team faces. That helps us make sure Aultrum fits into your existing way of working, and aligns with your goals.
