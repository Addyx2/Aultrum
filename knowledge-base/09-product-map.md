# 09 · Product map — how the system consumes the Layers

> How Peak, Gateway, the Flowstate platform, and future functions use the Layers and the qualifier engine.

---

## Single judgement, many surfaces

Every Aultrum product exists to make **quality delivered and consistent** more visible, more computable, and more acted upon.

```
                  THE JUDGEMENT
   "Is quality care delivered — and is it consistent?"
                          │
              ┌───────────┴───────────┐
              │  Aultrum Layers        │  six lenses on package health
              │  (qualifier engine)    │  research base → math → persona intelligence
              └───────────┬───────────┘
        ┌─────────────────┼──────────────────┐
        ▼                 ▼                  ▼
   Peak (retention)  Gateway (access)   Coordination & Co (future)
```

---

## The Flowstate platform (the container)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| Account | The organisation; whose package health is being assessed |
| Directory | People (carers, receivers, coordinators, key persons) — the subject of the qualifiers |
| Package | The unit of qualification — carer+coordinators+receiver as one judgement |
| Integrations | Where the observables come from (rota, clock-in, training, feedback) |

The platform is the funnel; value is added by the functions that act on package health.

---

## Peak — the retention function

**Consumes:** Competence, Shift Readiness, Rapport, Satisfaction, Purpose.
**Delivers:** carer-side consistency — recognition and rewards on the package.

| Receivable | Backed by lens signal |
|------------|----------------------|
| Cashable Points | Earned for completed quality-work signals (readiness, consistency) |
| Microrecognition | Badges for readiness streaks, rapport praise, competence milestones |
| Open Giftcard | Redeemable from earned points (flexible) |
| Early Rota | Rewards readiness/consistency with priority access to shifts |

**Judgement it serves:** *Will the carers stay?* — Purpose plus Shift Readiness (fatigue) feeding retention.

> Peak receivables are never a bribe for presence — rewards gate on measured quality (qualifier-backed), so every pound spent on incentives is a documented investment in quality.

---

## Gateway — the access function

**Consumes:** Compliance, Shift Readiness, Satisfaction.
**Delivers:** the first point of contact and the enquiry-to-package journey.

- Answers every inbound call 24/7 (never a missed family).
- Qualifies enquiries, books assessments, takes payments.
- Hands off into the platform, where the receiver becomes a package with qualifier lenses.
- Confidentiality and compliance routing (ZDR, audit trail).

**Judgement it serves:** *Does quality care even begin?* — a family's first experience is access.

---

## Coordination, Billing, Contracts & Co (future functions)

All future functions run on the same base and the same qualifier signal:

| Function | Consumes | Serves |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Coordination | Shift Readiness, Rapport | Schedules that respect readiness + preserve bonds |
| Billing | Compliance (records), ledger | Auditable, quality-linked financials |
| Contracts | Compliance, Satisfaction | Agreements that reflect actual delivered quality |
| (Future) Business health | All lenses, aggregate | Oversight across packages and agencies |

---

## Product principles (invariant)

1. **Every product is a function on the platform** — the base is the container; functions add the value.
2. **Every reward/pricing surface traces to a yardstick** — nothing is paid or praised without a measured basis.
3. **The qualifier unit is the Package** — individuals are seen through the package's lens.
4. **Persona Intelligence underpins all** — models refine from observation over time.
5. **Consistency is the product** — retention (carer, client, standards) is what the company sells in every form.
