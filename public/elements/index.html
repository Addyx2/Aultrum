PERSONA

The AI that understands care delivery.

Persona answers questions from your data and prepares work for your team, using the tools you choose to give it. Nothing changes until a person approves it.

  • checkWorks with your systems' tools, inside each person's permissions
  • checkEvery change waits for approval, and every action is audited
  • checkAutomations such as a morning briefing or a compliance watch
HOW IT WORKS

Your systems stay in charge.

Elements never writes to your data directly. It asks your system to act through its own tools, so your permissions, approvals and records still apply.
