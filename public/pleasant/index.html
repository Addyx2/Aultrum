ONE CLOSED LOOP

From a shift request to a paid invoice, without re-keying anything.

Most agencies stitch together a rota tool, a timesheet app, payroll software and a spreadsheet of invoices. Pleasant is one record from start to finish, so your margin is visible the moment a carer clocks in.

01
Request

A care home or provider asks for cover, by phone or through their Pleasant Link.

02
Match

Pleasant suggests carers who are free, cleared to work and right for the role.

03
Clock in

Carers clock in and out on their phone against the real shift.

04
Sign off

The client's named signatory approves the hours. No sign-off, no invoice.

05
Pay

PAYE, NI and pension calculated from approved hours, with RTI and BACS files ready.

06
Invoice

The client invoice comes from the same hours, with your margin on every shift.

OVERSIGHT

Know what needs you before the phone rings.

Oversight opens on the problems, most urgent first, each with the button that fixes it.

  • checkShifts starting in the next 48 hours with no carer, and unattended shifts
  • checkCarers who aren't cleared to work: missing or expired DBS or right to work
  • checkClients missing a rate card or a timesheet signatory
  • checkTimesheets to approve, and overdue invoices for admins
  • checkCover for the next 7 days, compared with the last 7
CLIENTPOINTE

Clients are organisations, not names on a list.

Every care home and provider has its units or branches, its contacts and its own rate card, so every shift is priced and signed off correctly.

  • checkRate cards by role: day, night, weekend, bank holiday and a flat sleep-in charge
  • checkRates for the whole client or for one unit
  • checkNamed signatories approve timesheets through a 24-hour Pleasant Link
  • checkFramework or PO references and payment terms on every invoice
PAYROLL AND BILLING

Pay carers and bill clients from the same approved hours.

Payroll and invoices come from one set of approved, client-signed timesheets, so they always agree.

  • checkUK PAYE, National Insurance and pension for every carer, with payslips
  • checkFPS-ready RTI file for HMRC, plus BACS STD18 and bank payment files
  • checkClient invoices with sleep-ins shown as flat charges, not hours
  • checkXero sales-invoice export for your accounts
PERSONA ASSIST

Assist does the work. You approve it.

Ask in plain English: fill tomorrow's open shifts, chase unsigned timesheets, set up a new care home. Assist finds the answer in your live data and prepares the change. Nothing happens until someone approves it.

  • checkEvery change waits on an Approve or Decline card, and is written to the audit log
  • checkWorks within each person's role. Admins choose which areas Assist can read or change
  • checkScheduled automations such as a morning briefing or a compliance watch, which can only read
  • checkSaved, searchable chat history, private to each user
