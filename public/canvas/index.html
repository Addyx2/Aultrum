Persona

Early access

The AI that understands care delivery. Persona answers questions from your data and prepares work for your team, using the tools you choose to give it. Nothing changes until a person approves it.

  • Works with your systems' tools, inside each person's permissions
  • Every change waits for approval, and every action is audited
  • Automations such as a morning briefing or a compliance watch

Elements

Gateway pilot · Peak soon

Ready-made care modules that slot into your software. Each one runs on Canvas and appears inside your own screens.

  • Gateway: one conversation layer for phone, SMS, WhatsApp and web chat. It recognises who is calling, routes to the right agent or person, and hands emergencies straight to a human.
  • Peak: points, streaks and rewards for carers, built from clock-ins and praise.

Inside your system · streamed from Canvas

How it works

Your systems stay in charge.

Persona and Elements never write to your data directly. They ask your system to act through its own tools, so your permissions, approvals and records still apply.

  1. 01

    Get your keys

    Create your organisation in Canvas and get API credentials for your own systems.

  2. 02

    Switch on what you need

    Add Persona, add Elements such as Gateway, or both, for the services you run.

  3. 03

    Connect your systems

    Show Elements inside your software, exchange signed events, and give Persona the tools it may use, under your own rules.

  4. 04

    Watch it work

    See every installation, conversation and event in one console, with delivery status and an audit trail.

Custom builds

Have an idea? We build it on Elements.

Tell us the workflow you wish you had. We build it as a custom module for your organisation, on the same foundations as Gateway and Peak, and it appears in Canvas alongside them.

  • Private to your organisation by default
  • The same security as every Element: signed links, signed events, full audit trail
  • Works with Persona, so your AI can use it too
