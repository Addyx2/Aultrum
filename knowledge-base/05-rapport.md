# 05 · Rapport — lens on package health

> **Lens:** Is the relationship between the care team and the receiver healthy, trusted, and consistent?

---

## Definition

Rapport observes the accumulated trust between a package and the person it cares for. It is measurable through continuity, satisfaction signals, and incident-free interaction — not through personality. A package with healthy rapport delivers care that the receiver experiences as a relationship, not a transaction.

## Mathematical grading model

> Working model — refine with the research base.

```
Rapport qualifier = Σ (continuity × satisfaction × interaction − disruption)

  continuity   = share of shifts held by the consistent core team
  satisfaction = slope of satisfaction/reply signals (trend, not level)
  interaction  = volume + valence of praise and positive notes
  disruption   = incidents, complaints, unexplained carer changes
  bond score   = learned per-pair value calibrated by persona intelligence
```

Rapport is graded at **pair and package level** — the carer-receiver bond is the unit, and package rapport is the aggregate.

---

## Persona Intelligence inputs

- Rota assignment history (who served whom, how often).
- Client/family satisfaction and praise signals.
- Incident and complaint records.
- The engine learns: *which pairs genuinely sustain trust, and what actually erodes it in this context* — the bond curve becomes individual, not generic.
