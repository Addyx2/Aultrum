What it is made of

Rapport accumulates from repeated successful interactions: consistency of carer, positive satisfaction signals, low incident rates, and open communication. Each visit either reinforces the bond or erodes it.

The measure is not a personality test. It is a record of durability: how many times has this pair worked together, how smoothly, and how positive were the outcomes?
