Privacy policy

How Aultrum Technologies Ltd (“Aultrum”, “we”) handles personal data on aultrum.co.uk and in Elements, Pleasant and Aultrum ID.

1. Who we are

Aultrum Technologies Ltd makes software for social care: Elements (Gateway and Peak), Pleasant, and Aultrum ID, the account that signs you in to them. For questions about your data, or to use your rights, email Wisdom@aultrum.co.uk.

2. Two roles

We handle personal data in two ways, and the rules differ:

  • As a controller, for our own purposes: our website, the accounts of people who use our products, sales conversations, and orders.
  • As a processor, for our customers: the care organisations that use Elements or Pleasant decide what data goes in about their staff, clients and callers, and we handle it only on their instructions, under our data processing agreement. If you are a carer, a client or a caller, the organisation you work with or contacted is responsible for that data. Contact them first; we will help them answer you.

3. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email, organisation, role, and a hashed password (we never store the password itself).
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, failed attempts, and IP addresses, used to keep accounts secure and to limit repeated attempts.
  • What our customers put in: for example carers’ and contacts’ names and phone numbers, Peak points and rewards, and in Pleasant shifts, timesheets, compliance records, payroll and invoices.
  • Gateway conversations: when someone calls, texts or chats with an organisation that uses Gateway, we keep the phone number or chat visitor code, the conversation as text, and a summary. Phone calls are turned into text; we do not keep audio recordings.
  • Website forms: what you send us when you book a demo, apply or contact us, or order merch (including a delivery address).

4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the products you or your organisation signed up for (contract).
  • To keep accounts and services secure, prevent abuse and fix faults (legitimate interests).
  • To reply to enquiries, arrange demos and send orders (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping financial records (legal obligation).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. How we use AI

Gateway and Persona use an AI model provided by Anthropic. The text of a conversation is sent to the model so it can reply. Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models.

AI never makes decisions about people on its own. Gateway answers questions and takes enquiries and messages for a person to follow up; Persona prepares work that a person must approve. We do not carry out solely automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on anyone.

7. International transfers

Some of these companies are based in the United States. When data leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and customer data: while the account is open. When a customer closes their account, we delete their data within 30 days, unless they ask us to return it first.
  • Sign-in records and rate-limit counters: up to 12 months.
  • Website enquiries and orders: up to 2 years, longer if the law requires (for example, financial records for 6 years).

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Links sent by email expire and work once. Embedded screens and webhooks are signed so they cannot be forged. Access is limited to the people who need it, and important actions are recorded in an audit log.

10. Cookies

Our apps use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. The website’s merch page remembers your basket in your browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

11. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. Email Wisdom@aultrum.co.uk; we reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

12. Children

Our products are for organisations and their staff. They are not meant for children under 16.

13. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and tell customers about significant changes by email.
