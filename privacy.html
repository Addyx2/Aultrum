Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1. Introduction

At Aultrum, we believe that empathy is foundational to effective care. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of the personal information entrusted to us by our research partners, care providers, and platform users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data Collection

We only collect information necessary to foster collaboration and improve our Empathetic Intelligence algorithms. This may include contact information provided via our Pioneer application form, technical usage data, and anonymized insights necessary for the development of proactive care ecosystems.

3. Data Security and Usage

Security is not an afterthought; it is woven into the fabric of the Flowstate framework. We employ state-of-the-art encryption protocols and adhere to strict ethical intelligence principles. Your data is never sold. It is exclusively utilized to refine our predictive algorithms in order to assist human decision-makers.

4. Your Rights

You maintain complete sovereignty over your data. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy team directly.
